Weekly rotation of guest lecturers speaking on a variety of topics such as psychology, theology, political philosophy, social justice, and environmental stewardship. The curriculum coordinated by [Dr. Syman Stevens](https://www.linkedin.com/in/symanstevens), and included a range of respected speakers such as Dr. Os Guiness, Andy and Catherine Crouch, and Curt Thompson. Each fellow was responsible for a year-long independent research project on a subject of their choosing, and a written thesis and multiple supporting essays. Parts of my writing have been published at [First Person Scholar](http://www.firstpersonscholar.com/a-review-of-miguel-sicarts-beyond-choices/) (<a href="#portfolio">Portfolio</a>)
