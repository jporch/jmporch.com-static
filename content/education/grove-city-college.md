ABET-accredited Computer Science department headed by [Dr. William Birmingham](http://www.gcc.edu/academics/SEM/computerscience/Pages/Faculty-Profiles.aspx?listid=16&listtype=Faculty&returl=http%3a%2f%2fwww.gcc.edu%3a80%2facademics%2fSEM%2fcomputerscience%2fPages%2fFaculty-.aspx&startrow=0). Advised by [Dr. Christiaan Gribble](http://www.rtvtk.org/~cgribble/). In addition to classes required for graduation, I pursued additional mathematics classes (Linear Algebra, Statistics Theory), summer research in mathematics and computer graphics (also under Dr. Gribble) [published](https://www.cs.unc.edu/~jheinly/publications/jgt2009-heinly.pdf) in the <cite>Journal of Graphics, GPU, and Game Tools</cite>. In addition to my Computer Science and math classes, I studied electives such as Technical Management, Creative Writing, and Fantasy Literature.
