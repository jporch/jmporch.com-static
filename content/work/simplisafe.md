At SimpliSafe I worked as a backend engineer for the Core Platform and User Systems teams. Our primary remit was maintaining core legacy JavaScript and TypeScript services that supported user and subscription services. We also pursued multiple modernization initiatives migrating functionality from the legacy monolith to a microservices model, primarily in Rust. We were one of the primary adopters of the internal events bus architecture, working to bring processes from server orchestration flows to event-driven ones built on top of Apache Kafka.
