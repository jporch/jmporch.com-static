As part of Grove City College's Sweezy Research Fellowship, I developed an integer ray-tracing pipeline and supporting research tools in C++ and Assembly for specialized hardware.

I co-authored the peer-reviewed paper <cite>Integer Ray Tracing</cite>, published in the <cite>Journal of Graphics, GPU, and Game Tools</cite>, and the research poster <cite>Toward Stream Filtered Ray Tracing on a DSP</cite>. (See <a href="#portfolio">Portfolio</a>.)
