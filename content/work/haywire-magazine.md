At [Haywire](https://haywiremag.com/), I wrote and edited videogame criticism, including the [Playing For Real](https://haywiremag.com/category/columns/playing-for-real/) column. I edited contributed writing from pitch toward publication, with particular attention to line editing that improved clarity while preserving each writer's voice and intent.

I assumed hosting and maintenance responsibility for the publication's WordPress site as its existing hosting arrangement became insufficient for ongoing platform needs. I designed and implemented a Codex-assisted transition from WordPress to static hosting to reduce maintenance demands and mitigate pressing security concerns.
